About

I am a PhD candidate in Quantitative Marketing at the Simon Business School, University of Rochester. I study dynamic competition in digital and retail markets: when firms release new products, how they price the ones already on the market, and how demand is reallocated when a rival enters or exits. My work combines reduced-form evidence with structural models of dynamic competition, estimated on large-scale platform data.

I will be on the academic job market in 2027–2028.

Research interests Digital Marketing · Marketing and Public Policy · Empirical Industrial Organization
