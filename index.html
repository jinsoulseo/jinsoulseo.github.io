About

I am a PhD candidate in Quantitative Marketing at the Simon Business School, University of Rochester. My work studies how firms and consumers respond when their market environment changes — platform interventions that alter what information buyers see, and structural breaks in local retail markets.

Research interests Digital Marketing · Marketing and Public Policy · Empirical Industrial Organization
